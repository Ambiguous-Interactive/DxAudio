Ducks

A duck lowers and filters the music while something holds it: dialogue, a menu, a cutscene. Every live duck composes into the most restrictive volume and filters, so systems duck and release without knowing about each other.

Hold and release

C#
MusicDuckLease lease = _music.Duck(_dialogueDuck, holder: this); // ... _music.Release(lease);

Duck returns a lease. Release releases that lease. A lease that was already released releases nothing.

Tip
A holder destroyed without releasing is released for it.

One or Many

One holds a state that is on or off, such as a menu. Many counts each holder, such as two characters speaking at once.

Warning
Releasing a One duck ends it for every holder.
